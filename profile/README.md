# AssetCool Recruitment

Welcome to the AssetCool Recruitment GitHub organization.

This organization is used to host assessments and practical exercises for candidates taking part in the AssetCool recruitment process.

If you have been invited here as a candidate, you are **not expected to browse or understand every repository in this organization**.

## 👋 Candidate? Start Here

Please begin with the assessment instructions provided to you by AssetCool.

Your instructions will explain:

- how the assessment process works;
- which repository or repositories are relevant to you;
- how to prepare any required tools or environment;
- the expected time allocation;
- submission requirements;
- use of AI tools;
- and any assessment-specific rules.

You should only work on repositories that have been explicitly assigned to you.

---

## How the Assessment Process Works

Depending on the role and stage of the recruitment process, you may be given access to one or more assessments or practical exercises.

These may involve different types of work depending on the role, for example:

- technical problem solving;
- practical exercises;
- written analysis;
- design tasks;
- troubleshooting;
- research;
- documentation;
- data analysis;
- system or process design;
- role-specific scenarios;
- or other practical tasks relevant to the position.

Not every candidate will receive the same assessment.

The repository assigned to you will contain the specific requirements for your task.

---

## Your Assessment Repository

For some assessments, a dedicated repository may be created specifically for you.

The repository name may include your GitHub username, for example:

```text
assessment-name_<github-username>
```

Your assigned repository will contain the files, documentation, resources, and instructions required for that assessment.

Always read the `README.md` inside the assigned repository before beginning.

---

## Before You Start

Before starting an assessment, please make sure that:

1. You have accepted any required GitHub organization or repository invitation.
2. You can access your assigned repository.
3. You have read the assessment instructions.
4. You have any required tools or software available.
5. You understand the suggested time allocation.

Some assessments may provide a preconfigured environment or additional resources.

Where these are provided, please use them as described in the assessment instructions.

---

## During the Assessment

Where Git is used, please **commit your work regularly**.

Your work history may form part of the assessment and can help us understand how you approached the task.

Where a suggested time allocation is provided, you may continue working beyond that time if you wish. However, work completed significantly beyond the allocated time is **not guaranteed to be included in the evaluation**.

Focus first on the core requirements before spending additional time on optional improvements.

---

## AI Tools

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in your assessment instructions, the use of AI-assisted tools is permitted.

If AI tools are used, you may be asked to provide the relevant prompts or describe how they were used.

You should be able to understand, explain, and discuss any work you submit.

AI should support your work rather than replace your own reasoning and judgement.

---

## Repository Rules

Unless your assessment explicitly states otherwise:

- Do not modify `.github/` or CI configuration.
- Do not modify assessment infrastructure.
- Do not attempt to access repositories that have not been assigned to you.
- Do not attempt to inspect private assessor or companion infrastructure.
- Keep your work within the repositories assigned to you.
- Where applicable, commit your work regularly rather than submitting everything in one final commit.

Some repositories in this organization are used internally by AssetCool to support assessment provisioning, automation, evaluation, and administration.

These repositories are **not part of the assessment unless explicitly stated otherwise**.

---

## What We Are Looking For

The exact evaluation criteria depend on the role and assessment, but may include:

- understanding of the problem;
- quality of reasoning;
- ability to work independently;
- judgement and prioritisation;
- clarity of communication;
- quality and completeness of the submitted work;
- ability to explain assumptions and trade-offs;
- attention to detail;
- and approach to unfamiliar problems.

A complete solution is not always required.

In many assessments, the way you investigate, prioritise, structure, and explain your work is as important as the final result.

---

## Need Help?

If you experience an issue with the assessment infrastructure, such as:

- you cannot access an assigned repository;
- required files appear to be missing;
- repository provisioning appears incomplete;
- an automated step has failed;
- or the provided environment cannot be accessed;

please contact the AssetCool representative who provided the assessment.

Unless specifically requested, do not attempt to modify the assessment infrastructure yourself.

Questions about the assessment itself should be handled according to the rules provided in the individual task.

---

## About AssetCool

AssetCool develops technology and engineering solutions for the electrical infrastructure sector.

Our assessments are designed to give candidates an opportunity to demonstrate their skills, judgement, problem-solving approach, and ability to work through realistic role-relevant tasks.

Good luck with your assessment.
